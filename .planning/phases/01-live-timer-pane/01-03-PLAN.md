<objective>
Make the pane stay accurate wherever and however the timer changes (SYNC-01..04), and make it reconnect by itself when TaskNotes is disabled, re-enabled or updated (SYNC-03, DISP-05 classification).

Purpose: plan 01-02 proved the read path on the happy path. This plan hardens each seam named in RESEARCH Pitfalls 1, 2, 4 and 5:
- the bridge must never re-attach a dead TaskNotes api
- the store must coalesce TaskNotes' duplicate `task-updated` events, keep the last good snapshot, and re-derive on deletes and renames that emit no runtime event
- the view must recompute from the wall clock after sleep and on becoming visible

Output: the hardened bridge, store and view; new `tests/bridge.test.ts`, `tests/store.test.ts`, `tests/fakes/fakeTaskNotes.ts` and `tests/fakes/fakeGateway.ts`; and wake-gap cases in `tests/readPath.test.ts`.
</objective>
